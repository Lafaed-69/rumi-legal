Rumi Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information Rumi may process, why it is used, how it is stored, and what choices users have regarding their data.

2. Information Rumi May Process

Rumi may process information necessary to provide its Discord bot features, including:

  • Discord User IDs;
  • Discord Server (Guild) IDs;
  • Discord Channel IDs when required for bot configuration;
  • usernames or display names when required for displaying bot features;
  • Rumi profile and progression data;
  • card collections and ownership data;
  • inventory items and frames;
  • virtual currency balances;
  • cooldowns and timestamps related to bot commands;
  • wishlists, tags, favorites, and other user-created bot settings;
  • trades, rewards, drops, and other game-related records;
  • server-specific Rumi settings;
  • technical information required to prevent abuse and maintain the service.

3. Why This Information Is Used

Information is processed only when reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, secure, and improve Rumi and its features.

This includes purposes such as:

  • maintaining user collections and inventories;
  • tracking virtual currency and progression;
  • processing trades, drops, rewards, and cooldowns;
  • saving user and server preferences;
  • preventing abuse, exploits, and unauthorized activity;
  • diagnosing errors and maintaining bot functionality.

4. Information Rumi Does Not Intentionally Collect

Rumi does not intentionally request or store passwords, Discord account credentials, payment card numbers, or authentication tokens belonging to users.

5. Data Sharing

Rumi does not sell personal data. Information may be processed by hosting, database, infrastructure, or other service providers only when necessary to operate the bot. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law.

6. Data Retention

Data may be retained for as long as it is reasonably necessary to provide Rumi's features, maintain game state, prevent abuse, resolve technical issues, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

7. Data Deletion Requests

Users may request deletion of personal data associated with their Discord account by contacting the developer through Rumi's official Discord support channels.

A Discord User ID may be required to identify the correct data. Certain limited information may be retained when reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, abuse prevention, or legal compliance.

8. Server Data

Server administrators may configure Rumi for their Discord servers. Some server-specific configuration data may remain stored while the service is in use and may be removed when no longer required.

9. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information processed by Rumi. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Discord

Rumi operates through Discord and is also subject to Discord's own terms, policies, and privacy practices. Rumi is not affiliated with or endorsed by Discord unless explicitly stated otherwise.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Rumi's features or data practices change. The date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.

12. Contact

Questions, privacy requests, and data deletion requests can be directed to the developer through Rumi's official Discord support channels.
